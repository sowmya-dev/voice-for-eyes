# Proposal: language-support

## Why
Users speak many Indian languages, with accents and mixed English, and must be answered in their own language.

## What Changes
- Support for 22 scheduled Indian languages and English.
- Per-utterance detection with confident switching and explicit language change.
- Reply language rules that drive recognition, templates and voice.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `language-support`: see [`specs/language-support/spec.md`](specs/language-support/spec.md).

### Modified Capabilities
None.

## Impact
New behaviour; no archived specs are affected. Source mapping: [`openspec/traceability.md`](../../traceability.md).
